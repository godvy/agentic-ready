Set up SAML single sign-on

Available on the Business plan. You need Owner access in Waddle and admin access in your identity provider (Okta, Entra ID, Google).

1. Create a SAML app in your identity provider

SettingValue
ACS (reply) URLhttps://auth.waddle.app/saml/acs
Entity ID (audience)urn:waddle:sp
NameID formatEmailAddress

2. Map attributes

  • NameID: the user's email
  • groups: the user's group names. Required: without it, everyone joins as a Guest.

3. Connect Waddle

In Waddle go to Settings → Security → SSO, paste your IdP metadata URL and click Save.

4. Enforce SSO safely

  1. Turn on Allow password fallback for Owners first. Otherwise a broken IdP config locks every admin out.
  2. Click Test SSO and sign in from a private window.
  3. Only after the test succeeds, toggle Require SSO.
